Auth pane

The section bar reads "Auth" whatever the scheme. The pane is two columns split by a 1 pt rule: a 240 pt left column with "Auth type", the pop-up and a paragraph that says what is true, and the fields on the right. Below 600 pt of pane width the columns stack and the rule turns horizontal (the last row). The third card has an Authorization row in Headers, so the paragraph adds that the row is sent instead.
