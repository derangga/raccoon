16 · Breadcrumb

The Breadcrumb names where the selected Tab's Request lives, from its Collection down to its own name, on the editor's background between the tab strip and the URL bar. Ancestors are textSecondary, the Request's name is text semibold, the chevrons are textTertiary. Clicking any segment, its own included, shows the sidebar, expands the ancestors, clears a filter that would hide the row, and selects and scrolls to it. Focus stays in the Tab, no Tab opens and nothing else changes. Below: the result of a click, the middle collapsed in a narrow content area, a very long name, a Draft, and the segment states.
